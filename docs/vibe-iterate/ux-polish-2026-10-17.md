## The Desk on a full desktop

What the Desk knows about what is under it: the Dock (`Desk/DockClearance.swift`, `DockFollower.swift:184` → margins at `DeskArrangement.swift:227-233`), the notch (`DeskView.swift:94-98`) and other apps' windows (`DeskController.swift:441,548`). Nothing about Finder's icons or the Desktop folder. The window sits one level below normal (`DeskController.swift:280`), above the icon layer, so text always lands on file names.

Readability today: a shared ink with opacity 0.92 and a black shadow (`DeskView.swift:473-482`, toggled by "Drop shadow under the clock text"); the message gets a glow in its own color instead (`DeskMessageLine.swift:57-58`), which adds no contrast on light icons; several pieces draw at 0.45–0.85 opacity (`DeskView.swift:260,297,301,327,348,355`); the only thing behind text is a 0.015-opacity hit plate (`DeskSupport.swift:150`). "Clock and message take clicks" defaults on and costs clicks on icons beneath (`DeskHitTest.swift:321-333`). First-run layout: message top-left, clock and meters bottom-left (`DeskSupport.swift:70,106`); Finder fills from the top-right, so the default already sits on the quieter side.

## The shape of Mods & Config for a first-timer

On first open (no kept mods, no proposals, one folder, meters not installed): the summary card ("N mods / N plugins / N enabled plugins / 1 folder" and a sentence about switches that don't exist on the page), the three tabs, then on Catalog the long intro, the "What a new session starts with" panel, the filter and sort toolbar, "Yours", and only then the meters card, named `sanduhr-meters` with a green "Available" badge and two link buttons. Nothing says "Meters above the prompt" and nothing offers a switch.

Two structural moves cover most of the trust-5 rows:

1. **A first-run header above the tabs**: "Meters above the prompt", one row per folder with its On switch (consent sheet unchanged), and Studio…; the tabs sit under a "More: mods and config" disclosure that opens by itself once the user has a kept mod, a proposal or a second folder. Closes #1, #2, #3, #4, #6, #34.
2. **Progressive disclosure and one word per concept**: hide the filters, sort, Expand/Collapse, the Seat picker and Compare until 2+ folders or ~10+ cards; one "…" menu per row and per seat holding the rare actions, with Remove, Dismiss and Undo Import confirmed and marked destructive; "folder" everywhere (drop "seat" from the UI), "mod" and "plugin" with no "Extensions", "On / Off / Not installed" for Sanduhr's own mod, the "Side" label shown, and the names table matching the screen. Closes #5, #10–#17, #20, #23, #24, #26.

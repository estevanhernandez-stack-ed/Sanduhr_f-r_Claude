## English

```
Version 4.5

- Set up Sanduhr: a new install opens one screen. Pick a desktop clock,
  message and weather, drawers for your files, your Claude usage, or any
  mix, and change it any time from the tray. The desktop and the drawers
  need no Claude account.
- Drawers: panels on the desktop, each a real folder inside a Drawers
  folder on your Desktop. Built-in rules sort screenshots, pictures,
  documents, installers, archives, shortcuts and files unchanged for a
  month. Nothing moves until you press Apply on the review, Undo puts it
  back, and turning drawers off returns every file. Drawers only move
  files; they never delete or rename one.
- Drawer looks: Solid, Glass, Outline or Bare, with opacity. Drawers
  snap to each other and the screen edges, keep clear of your desktop
  icons, and Tidy drawers lines them up.
- Design it with an AI chat: copy a prompt into the chat you use, paste
  its answer back, and preview it on your Desk. Sanduhr sends nothing to
  the chat.
- With usage off, the tray shows the hourglass.

Nothing new leaves your PC: 4.5 adds no network destination.

Known issue: if the update won't install (error 0x80073D02), restart
Windows and try again.

"Claude" and "claude.ai" are trademarks of Anthropic PBC, used
nominatively. Sanduhr für Claude is an independent third-party tool, not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Anthropic PBC.
```

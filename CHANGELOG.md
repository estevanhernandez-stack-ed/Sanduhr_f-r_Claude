## v2.9.0-mac — 2026-10-07

- **Claude Code's hooks no longer launch Sanduhr.** The notch glow hooks used to open a `sanduhr://` link, which macOS hands to the installed app and starts it if it isn't running: with a second copy running (a dev build), every finished turn launched the installed one, and a hook could bring back a Sanduhr you had quit. They now post a system notification (`notifyutil -p com.626labs.sanduhr.claude-code.waiting` or `.done`) that reaches whichever Sanduhr is running and does nothing when none is. Settings, Integrations shows hooks installed by an earlier version as Outdated; Install updates them in place and Remove still takes them out exactly. The Stop hook hands over background work only while Sanduhr runs, and a report left from a time Sanduhr wasn't running is dropped unread. The `sanduhr://claude-code` link still works.
- **On special days: stack, take turns or scroll.** Settings, Message has a new "On special days" choice for how a date's lines share the Desk with the day's line. **Stack** (the default) shows them all, the date's lines on top. **Take turns** shows one line at a time, crossfading from the date's lines to the day's line and round again. **Scroll** moves them up one after another like a slow ticker. Pick how long each line shows: 5 s, 10 s, 30 s, 1 min or 5 min. The message keeps one size while lines change, so nothing beside it moves; each line keeps its own effects, and a `{write}` line writes itself in each time it comes round. The notch's Message shows the same line at the same moment. With Reduce Motion lines swap without a fade or glide, and nothing changes while the Desk is covered or the screens sleep. On a day without special lines nothing changes at all. The Message preview shows your choice live.
- **Changed: special days add to the day instead of replacing it.** A date line (`10-31: happy halloween.`) used to be the only line the Desk showed on that date. Now it shows above the day's usual line, so a birthday no longer hides your "good vibes only" (pinned or not), and every line for that date shows (two birthdays on one date both show; more than three take turns hourly, three at a time). The date's lines draw a little smaller than the usual line, each with its own effects; the notch's Message shows the first of them on that date. If you relied on a date line replacing the day's line, this is a deliberate change.
- **Fixed: weekday lines took turns badly.** Several lines for one weekday now come round in turn, one each week; before, seven Friday lines always showed the same one, and other counts repeated or skipped. Weekday lines still replace the every-day lines on their day, unless you switch on Settings, Message's new "Mix every-day lines in on days with their own line", which lets them take turns together. Each row in the editor now says what happens to it ("Fridays · takes turns with 2 others", "Every day · steps aside on days with their own line"), and "Today:" lists everything the Desk draws today. Claude's Desk message tools know the new rules and suggest birthdays and holidays as date lines.
- **A Message editor anyone can use.** Settings, Message is now a list of your lines, each drawn as the Desk draws it. Edit a line to set **When** (every day, a weekday, or a date), its **Text**, and its look with buttons instead of tags: **Color** (the Desk's, one color, or a gradient of 2 to 4 colors, with eight named palettes: synthwave, sunset, ocean, aurora, ember, bubblegum, toxic and gold), **Glow** (on, off, as the Desk), **Size** (0.5 to 2 times), **Letters** (bold, italic, small caps, script, fraktur and the rest) and **Motion** (none, write in, shimmer, sweep). The row's picture changes as you do. Add Line, Duplicate, Delete, drag to reorder (or Move Up and Move Down), Pin a line so it stays, and the daily or hourly rotation all sit on the page; Save writes the file. Fridays' "ship it." in a script sunset gradient with a sweep is a few clicks, and the file holds `Fri: {ink:#ff7e5f,#feb47b,#ffd86f} {font:script} {sweep} ship it.` **Edit as text…** still shows the file itself, now with the tag reference beside it; both views edit the same file and switching keeps your unsaved edits. A file you wrote by hand opens in the list and saves back exactly as it was when you change nothing; lines the editor can't represent (a tag Sanduhr doesn't know, two motions on one line) show as written and stay that way, and notes and blank lines keep their place. Claude's suggestions still arrive in the card at the top, and **Ask Claude** has an example prompt to copy.
- **EsteFont Pro, the Desk's new handwriting.** EsteFont Pro (Regular and Bold, version 3.000) by Estevan Hernandez now ships inside Sanduhr beside EsteFont 26, loaded for Sanduhr only like it (nothing is installed on your Mac). A new install's Desk draws in EsteFont Pro, the clock's time in its Bold face. If you already picked a Desk font it stays, EsteFont 26 included; if your Desk was drawing in EsteFont 26 because you never picked one, it keeps EsteFont 26; a picked font that is no longer installed now falls back to EsteFont Pro. Both font lists (Settings, Desk, Look and Settings, Widget, Look) put EsteFont Pro first and EsteFont 26 right after it, and the widget's semibold and bold text uses the Bold face of either. Settings, About credits both, and the third-party notices carry EsteFont Pro's license.
- **Letter styles and a light sweep for Desk messages.** Two new tags for a line's text. `{font:small-caps}` draws it in a letter style: `bold`, `italic`, `bold-italic` and `small-caps` in your Desk font itself (its Bold face, a slant, capitals with the lowercase letters at x-height size), and `sans`, `mono`, `double-struck`, `script` and `fraktur` as Unicode letters, the same nine styles the statusline offers (`{font:smallcaps}` works too). `{sweep}` sends a small white light, three letters wide, across the line once when it appears, as the now-playing mod's title does; `{sweep:30}` repeats it every 30 seconds (2 to 3600). For example `{font:smallcaps} {sweep} showtime.` With Reduce Motion nothing sweeps; the sweep also rests while the Desk is covered or the screens sleep, and costs nothing between runs. The notch now shows a message line without its tags, in its letter style. Settings, Message's Replay sweeps a `{sweep}` line at once. Claude learns both tags from the MCP server's Desk message tools, and a tag Sanduhr doesn't know still shows as written.
- **More places on the Desk, your order, a size per piece.** Settings, Desk, Layout now offers eight places: the four corners plus Top center, Bottom center, Middle left and Middle right. Top center sits just below the notch, and below the island when it's on. A new Order list shows each place's pieces top to bottom: drag a piece up or down to reorder it, or onto a piece in another place to move it there (VoiceOver has Move Up and Move Down). Each piece has its own size, 60% to 160%, scaled from the clock size in Look (the message from its own size), and it keeps that size when it moves. The map at the top of the page draws every piece where it will sit, in its order and at its size, with the notch and the Dock, and changes as you do. The Dock clearance and the click areas cover the new places. Your current layout looks exactly as before, and a layout string with a place Sanduhr doesn't know puts that piece where it normally goes instead of hiding it. Margins stay the same for every place. Pieces in different places never draw over each other: with something at Top or Bottom center, the sides keep to the room the center leaves (a long message wraps and shrinks short of it), and a Middle left or right piece sits between its side's top and bottom pieces, which push it instead of covering it.
- **A Mods page: see every Claude Code mod you have.** Settings, Mods (under Integrations) lists every mod and plugin each Claude Code folder loads: the plugin folders in its settings, its installed plugins, plugin folders in its skills folder, and mods a session made (which load in that session only). Each shows its name, version, description, where it loads from, whether it is On, Off or Missing, and what it touches: what it draws in Claude Code (a band above the prompt, a pane, a status entry, toasts, slash commands, by name) and whether its code runs programs, uses the network or reads and writes files. A mod's surfaces are drawn as a sketch in a terminal frame, blocks in place of its content. Sanduhr finds all this by reading files; no mod runs. **Check** asks Claude Code's own validator (`claude plugin validate`), which reads a mod without running it, and shows a risk card: whether Claude Code would load it, its errors and warnings, what it hooks and calls, and a low, medium or high risk with the reasons (programs, the network, writing files and steering Claude's tool calls are high). It gives up after 10 seconds; without Claude Code's command line the button says why it is off. A card on top counts mods, plugins, how many are on, and folders. The page is read-only: it never turns a mod on or off or edits a settings file. Backup copies of a Claude Code folder (`*.config-backup-*`) no longer show up as folders here or in Integrations.

- **A live preview in every settings pane.** Each Settings page that changes something you see now opens with a picture of it: the notch island at its true proportions with its wings, the strip, the camera and mic indicators and a Test Glow that glows the card with the real notch (a Mac without a notch shows the top tab); a map of the screen with every Desk piece in its corner and the Dock on its edge (Layout); a Desk corner with the message, clock and meters in your fonts, sizes, ink and glow (Look); the meters with their warnings and hidden limits (Meters); today's message with its effects and a Replay for `{write}` and `{shimmer}` (Message); a wing playing, paused and with nothing playing, and the Desk line (Now Playing); the widget's cards in your theme and font (Widget Look, Pacing & Focus); the menu bar item as it will read, both readings for Rotate (General); and Sanduhr's statusline in a terminal frame with a watcher (Integrations). Each is drawn by the same views as the real thing and changes as you change a setting. Your own numbers, meetings, track and watchers show where Sanduhr has them; until then the card shows made-up ones and says "Sample". Reduce Motion stills them, VoiceOver reads each in one sentence, and nothing captures the screen.
- **Camera and mic indicators on the notch.** A red recording dot while a camera is in use, and an orange mic glyph while any app uses the microphone, set in Settings, Desk, Notch, Camera and mic (both off by default). **Show the red dot** is Never, For cameras without a visible light, or Always: a MacBook's camera has its own green light, so the middle choice shows the dot only for an external or Continuity camera, or the built-in one with the lid closed. The mic has its own switch. They sit on the island beside the camera (left or right, your pick), which grows to fit them so the wings keep their room; pick **Camera and mic** for a wing or the strip under the camera to show them there instead. With the island off, or on a screen without a notch, they show in a small black tab at the top. They fade in and out over a quarter second, and the dot breathes gently (switch it off; with Reduce Motion it holds still and nothing fades). When they go, the island and its wings return exactly to where they were. They are indicators only: a click shows what is in use ("Camera in use", "Microphone in use") and Indicator Settings…, and Sanduhr never mutes or changes a device. Sanduhr learns only that a camera or the default microphone is in use, never which app or anything captured; no permission is asked, nothing is recorded, logged or saved. The mic follows the default input device as it changes (a headset connecting). They run while Desk does and sit alongside watchers and now playing.
- **Fixed: a failed watcher looked like a camera light.** A failed watcher now shows a red exclamation mark in a triangle instead of a red dot, so the camera indicator is the only red dot in Sanduhr. Waiting on you stays amber; VoiceOver still says "failed".
- **A signed-in account no longer offers "Sign In Again".** On an account's page the button reads Replace Sign-In… while the account works, and Sign In Again… only once its session has expired, next to "Session expired", so a working account never looks signed out.

## v2.8.0-mac — 2026-10-07

- **Watchers: live eyes on work in flight.** A watcher is a small live card on the notch or the Desk: a state dot (running, waiting on you, passed, failed), the title, the time so far, the progress when there is a total and a one-line note. Two switches in Settings, Integrations, Watchers, both off by default. **Let agents show watchers** lets Claude Code put one up through the MCP server's new `watch_start`, `watch_update` and `watch_end` (a CI run, a deploy, a long test run), with an https link a click opens; off, the server refuses them. **Show Claude Code's background work** turns a background shell, monitor, subagent or workflow into a watcher on its own, ending when it leaves the session's list; the notch glow hooks carry it (Install updates an older copy, shown as Outdated), handing over only each task's kind, status and short description, never a command or anything from the conversation, and Sanduhr deletes it as it reads it. Place watchers like everything else: Watchers for a notch wing or the strip under the camera in Settings, Notch (the most urgent one: its full line once, scrolling through when it doesn't fit, then a short "PR 140 · 4/12 +1"; the intro plays again when the top watcher or its state changes, never with Reduce Motion; agents can pass `short` to `watch_start` for that name), or the Watchers element in Settings, Desk, Layout (up to four). Waiting on you pulses and glows the notch once; passed shows a check and fades; failed stays until you dismiss it; a watcher that goes quiet greys as lost touch. Two-finger click for Dismiss, Dismiss All and Watcher Settings…. A new **Work account** switch in Settings, Accounts, Data marks an account's Claude Code folder as work: its watchers (and any an agent flags as work) hide in demo mode. Watchers live in memory only. The MCP server is now 0.5.0-mac.
- **Pick the segments you keep when combining statuslines.** Combine no longer means all or nothing. The sheet runs your statusline once against sample data and shows its segments as chips, line by line, cut on the separator it uses (`|`, `·`, `│`, `•`, powerline arrows or wide spaces), which is shown and can be changed per line; Sanduhr's own segments are chips too: session, weekly, weekly reset, and now context and model, read from Claude Code. Click a chip to keep or drop it and the preview shows the final line as it will look. Your segments are recognized by how they start, not where they sit, so one that comes and goes (a git branch outside a repository) never moves the others, and segments your statusline adds later stay unless you turn that off. What remains is rejoined with your own separators and colors (powerline arrows redrawn between new neighbors); a line you drop entirely goes, and Sanduhr's meters always show. ### Fixed

- `.github/workflows/windows-release.yml` installed Inno Setup via chocolatey
  with a pinned `--version=6.2.2`, which failed on `windows-latest` runners
  because they ship a newer (6.7.1) pre-install. Replaced with an idempotent
  presence check that only installs when missing.
- `Sanduhr.iss` hardcoded `#define MyAppVersion "2.0.0"` would override
  the `/DMyAppVersion=X.Y.Z` flag that `build.ps1` passes from
  `pyproject.toml`, producing `Sanduhr-Setup-v2.0.0.exe` regardless of the
  real version. Wrapped in `#ifndef` so `/D` wins.
